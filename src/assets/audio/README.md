# Sound assets

- `mud02.ogg` — used for dirt impacts and breaks.
- `stone01.ogg` — used for stone impacts (stone breaks use `gravel.ogg`).
- `bonus-pop.mp3` — plays when ore or coal is mined.
- `ore-impact.ogg` — Kenney's `footstep_carpet_003.ogg`, used when striking ore.
- `gravel.ogg` — used when breaking ore, coal, or stone.
- Positive bonus pickups currently use the synthesized reward sound.

Source: [Different steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud](https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud)
Author: TinyWorlds. OpenGameArt lists this pack under CC0.

Mining sound: [Minecraft pop.mp3](https://freesound.org/people/mdeliushussain/sounds/688737/) by mdeliushussain, listed on Freesound under CC0.

Ore impact: [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) by Kenney. Kenney lists the pack under CC0.

Background music: `Loop_The_Bards_Tale.mp3` is a compressed copy of the user-supplied `Loop_The_Bards_Tale.wav`, used as a looping music bed. The source URL, author, and license were not supplied in this conversation; no license is asserted here.

The mining pop has its leading silence trimmed. Original downloaded texture-pack experiments were reverted and are not part of this asset set.
