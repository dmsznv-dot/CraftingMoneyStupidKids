# Sound assets

- `mud02.ogg` — used for dirt impacts and breaks.
- `stone01.ogg` — used for stone impacts (stone breaks use `gravel.ogg`).
- `bonus-pop.mp3` — plays when ore or coal is mined.
- `ore-impact.ogg` — Kenney's `footstep_carpet_003.ogg`, used when striking ore.
- `gravel.ogg` — used when breaking ore, coal, or stone.
- Positive bonus pickups currently use the synthesized reward sound.

Source: [Different steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud](https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud)
Author: TinyWorlds. OpenGameArt lists this pack under CC0.

Mining sound: [Minecraft pop.mp3](https://freesound.org/people/mdeliushussain/sounds/688737/) by mdeliushussain, listed on Freesound under CC0.

Ore impact: [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) by Kenney. Kenney lists the pack under CC0.

The mining pop has its leading silence trimmed. Original downloaded texture-pack experiments were reverted and are not part of this asset set.

- `pick-break.mp3` — user-supplied crate break sound, plays when the pick breaks and the result screen appears. The source page and license were not supplied; no license is asserted here.

- `The_Old_Tower_Inn.mp3` — background music by RandomMind, [source on OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-the-old-tower-inn), listed as CC0. Part of the background playlist with two-second crossfades.

- `The_Bards_Tale.mp3` — full version of The Bard's Tale by RandomMind, [source on OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-the-bards-tale), listed as CC0. Part of the background playlist.

- `Minstrel_Dance.mp3` — background music by RandomMind, [source on OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-minstrel-dance), listed as CC0.

- `revive.mp3` — shortened, gently faded excerpt of [AHHHH](https://freesound.org/people/random_intruder/sounds/392172/) by random_intruder, Freesound lists CC0. Played on resurrection; the original is not included.

- `workshop.mp3` — a 1.25-second edit of [Hammer hitting an anvil](https://freesound.org/people/draftcraft/sounds/434339/) by draftcraft, listed on Freesound under CC0. Two hammer strikes and a short metallic sound play when opening the workshop. Original recording was supplied by the user.
